# Commercialization Launch Kit

**Goal**: sell the first version within one week as a service-backed product, then use AICO to iterate the SME Agent into a repeatable software/service system.

## Product wedge

Do not sell a generic "SME Agent" in the first week. Sell a concrete, reviewable outcome:

> AI business diagnosis for small businesses: turn orders, customers, inventory, ads, finance notes, and scattered spreadsheets into a traceable diagnosis report and a small set of questions the owner can keep asking.

The first offer is human-reviewed. The product promise is "AI-assisted analysis with evidence and human verification", not "fully automatic business management".

## First purchasable SKU

### Title draft

中小企业 AI 经营诊断｜收入下降/客户流失/库存积压/广告 ROI 分析｜老板数据助手

### Buyer

- Taobao/Tmall/Pinduoduo/Douyin store owner.
- Local retail or service owner with order/customer/inventory data.
- Small operations team that has spreadsheets but no dedicated analyst.
- Agency or consultant that repeatedly prepares operating reports for clients.

### Initial packages

| Package | Launch price | Promise | Delivery |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Entry diagnosis | 199 RMB | Determine whether the customer's data is analyzable | 1-page issue map and next-step checklist |
| Standard diagnosis | 699 RMB | Answer one focused operating question from up to three tables | Data checklist, metric definitions, diagnosis report, follow-up questions |

Push the 199 RMB and 699 RMB packages first. The 1999 RMB package is an upsell after fit and trust are established.

## Week-one acceptance

The week-one version is acceptable only if all are true:

1. A buyer can understand the offer from the product page without knowing what an agent is.
2. The order page states what data the buyer must provide and what is out of scope.
3. The delivery report includes evidence, assumptions, and human-review notes.
4. AICO can track daily work: content, customer conversations, product changes, delivery risks, and next actions.
5. SME Agent can support at least metadata grounding and report assembly from structured inputs, even if ingestion is still semi-manual.

## Non-promises

Do not claim:

- Fully automatic SaaS.
- Guaranteed revenue growth.
- Replacement for licensed accounting, tax, legal, or financial advice.
- Accurate diagnosis without source data.
- Direct platform integration before platform credentials and rules are verified.

Use:

- "AI-assisted operating diagnosis."
- "Human-reviewed."
- "Traceable assumptions and evidence."
- "Suitable for teams with order/customer/inventory/ad data but no analyst."

## Product loop

```text
Xiaohongshu pain article
  -> private chat qualification
  -> order / trial intent
  -> data checklist
  -> SME Agent metadata + knowledge + report workflow
  -> human review
  -> diagnosis delivery
  -> anonymized case
  -> new Xiaohongshu article and product backlog
```

## AICO team roles

- **Lead**: daily priority and tradeoff owner.
- **Product Manager**: converts customer conversations into offer, scope, and backlog.
- **Growth Writer**: turns customer pain points into Xiaohongshu posts and reply scripts.
- **Delivery Analyst**: creates diagnosis report drafts from customer data.
- **Metadata Engineer**: maintains terms, metrics, dimensions, entities, and source tables.
- **Knowledge Engineer**: ingests business notes, policies, FAQ, and case learnings.
- **Runtime Engineer**: builds import, retrieval, report, and agent-loop capabilities.
- **Challenger**: attacks claims, risks, overpromises, and weak evidence.
- **Reviewer**: checks buyer-facing copy and delivery reports before release.
