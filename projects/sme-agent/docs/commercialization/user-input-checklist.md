# User Input Checklist

These are the inputs needed from the human owner to make the product commercially sellable, not just technically interesting.

## Platform and store inputs

- Taobao store URL or screenshots of the current shop backend.
- The category candidates available when publishing a service product.
- Whether the shop can publish virtual/service products today.
- Any platform prompts about qualification, banned words, deposit, delivery method, or after-sales rules.
- Whether the target is ordinary Taobao listing first, Qianniu service market, or both.
- Screenshots of the product publishing flow, especially title, category, SKU, delivery, after-sales, and detail-page fields.

## Offer inputs

- Preferred first package price is fixed for launch unless changed by the owner: 199 / 699 / 1999 RMB.
- Whether the first 3 customers can be discounted in exchange for anonymized case permission.
- Industries you can credibly serve first: e-commerce, local retail, B2B sales, service business, agency clients.
- Your strongest personal proof: prior BI/data/AI/project experience, screenshots, articles, or past cases that can be shown.

## Customer-data sample inputs

Provide synthetic or real anonymized samples. Remove names, phone numbers, addresses, IDs, and transaction secrets.

Minimum useful files:

- Order table: order id, date, amount, product, channel, customer id, region, status, refund marker.
- Product table: product id, category, price, cost, inventory, gross margin if available.
- Customer table: customer id, first purchase date, last purchase date, segment or source if available.
- Ad table if applicable: date, channel, spend, clicks, orders, GMV.
- Business notes: revenue recognition rule, refund rule, channel definitions, region definitions.

## Content inputs

- Xiaohongshu account link or screenshots.
- Existing bio, avatar, nickname, and any posts.
- Whether your persona should be "AI经营诊断师", "数据分析顾问", "老板AI助手搭建者", or another position.
- Topics you are comfortable discussing publicly.
- Topics you do not want to mention.

## Delivery preferences

- Preferred report format: Markdown, PDF, Word, or Feishu/Docs.
- Whether delivery should happen through Taobao chat, WeChat, email, or downloadable file.
- Maximum time you can spend per entry diagnosis and standard diagnosis.
- Whether you are willing to do a 30-minute call with standard-package buyers.
