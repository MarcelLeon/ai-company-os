# Challenge log for commercialization

This file records adversarial reflection. Update it whenever Lead or Challenger changes the commercial plan.

## Round A — Positioning

- Original assumption: sell a general SME Agent.
- Challenge: a general agent is hard to understand, hard to price, and risky to deliver in one week.
- Revision: sell a human-reviewed AI business diagnosis service, then turn repeated delivery patterns into product capabilities.

## Round B — Automation

- Original assumption: maximize automation before launch.
- Challenge: customer data will be messy, metric semantics will be ambiguous, and a wrong answer damages trust.
- Revision: automate intake classification and report outline only; keep final business conclusions human-reviewed.

## Round C — Channel

- Original assumption: launch directly through Qianniu service-market style packaging.
- Challenge: platform category, service-market entry, and AI-service wording may require manual account checks and review.
- Revision: prepare both paths: publish a normal service listing if allowed by the existing shop, while the user checks Qianniu/service-market requirements in the actual backend.

## Round D — Growth

- Original assumption: Xiaohongshu posts should directly push buyers to Taobao.
- Challenge: aggressive external conversion can hurt account safety and trust.
- Revision: posts teach one business framework, comments/DM qualify pain, Taobao is introduced only after fit and consent.
