# One-week commercial launch plan

## Sellable product boundary

First version sells a human-reviewed service, not an autonomous SaaS:

> 中小企业 AI 经营诊断：把订单、客户、库存、广告或财务导出数据整理成一份可追溯经营诊断报告，并沉淀后续可问的指标口径。

## Week-one acceptance

- A Taobao listing can be published from the prepared copy.
- A Xiaohongshu account can publish one pain-point post per day.
- A prospect can answer the intake questions in less than 10 minutes.
- SME Agent can classify the lead as not-ready, intro diagnosis, or standard report.
- SME Agent can generate a report outline that preserves evidence IDs and disclaimers.
- A human reviews every customer-facing report before delivery.

## Day-by-day execution

| Day | Commercial work | Product work | AICO loop |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Publish first Xiaohongshu post and prepare Taobao listing draft | Freeze service boundary and intake questions | `/ask lead` reviews launch scope |
| 2 | Build one anonymized demo story | Generate sample report outline from revenue grounding | Challenger attacks over-promises |
| 3 | Publish Taobao service item if category checks pass | Run intake assessment tests | Reviewer checks listing claims |
| 4 | DM and qualify 10 prospects | Add customer project folder template manually | Lead chooses one delivery sample |
| 5 | Publish three pain posts from prospect objections | Produce first report from sample data | Tester verifies evidence/disclaimer gates |
| 6 | Recruit 1-3 seed users at low price/free for testimonial rights | Record case-study metadata and missing data patterns | `/daily` captures lessons |
| 7 | Tighten pricing, FAQ, and refund boundary | Turn first case into reusable template | `/weekly` decides next sprint |

## Pricing ladder

- 引流诊断：199 元，交付 1 页经营问题体检和是否适合 AI 诊断的判断。
- 标准报告：699 元，交付数据整理、指标口径说明、经营诊断报告和 3 个后续追问。
- 深度助手体验版：1999 元，作为人工咨询升级，不在第一周承诺全自动交付。

## Do not promise in week one

- Do not promise fully autonomous analysis.
- Do not promise revenue lift, cost reduction, tax, legal, finance, or investment advice.
- Do not promise direct connection to every ERP/CRM/e-commerce platform.
- Do not accept data without explicit analysis authorization.
- Do not publish customer cases without anonymized permission.

## Evidence to record

- Listing URL or screenshot.
- Published Xiaohongshu links and daily metrics.
- Lead intake forms and assessment output.
- Report outline, human review notes, and final delivered report.
- Questions prospects asked before paying or refusing.
