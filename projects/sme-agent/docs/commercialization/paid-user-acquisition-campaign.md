# Paid-user acquisition campaign

This is the first commercial campaign for SME Agent. Its job is not brand
awareness. Its job is to bring back qualified merchants who may pay for a
human-reviewed live-commerce diagnosis.

Use [`first-post-launch-pack.md`](first-post-launch-pack.md) for the first
public post and immediate reply scripts. Use
[`growth-operations-playbook.md`](growth-operations-playbook.md) for the
post-publication operating cadence, channel expansion, nurture sequence, and
weekly review.

## Campaign objective

Get the first `10-30` qualified leads from Xiaohongshu and WeChat, then convert
the best `3-5` into paid diagnosis orders.

Primary conversion path:

```text
Xiaohongshu pain post
  -> comment or DM keyword
  -> WeChat 17610788906, remark: 直播诊断
  -> field checklist
  -> free field triage
  -> 199 RMB mini diagnosis or 699 RMB standard diagnosis
  -> anonymized case
  -> next Xiaohongshu post
```

Do not send, post, or add WeChat contacts automatically without the human
owner's explicit approval. Drafts are safe; external publication and outbound
messages need confirmation.

## Positioning

One sentence:

> 我用 AI 帮中小直播商家把后台数据翻译成人话：不是只看 GMV，而是定位哪场直播、哪个 SKU、哪个支付口径把钱拖下来了。

What to sell first:

- `0 RMB`: field triage. The merchant sends only table headers or a redacted
  sample. We decide whether the question is analyzable.
- `199 RMB`: mini diagnosis. One live-room export pair, one focused question,
  one-page diagnosis.
- `699 RMB`: standard diagnosis. Field mapping, metric definitions,
  live-session/SKU attribution, and a review voice note.
- `1999 RMB`: deep assistant trial. Two-week tracking and follow-up questions,
  sold only after the merchant trusts the standard diagnosis.

Do not sell:

- guaranteed GMV growth;
- a fully automatic SaaS;
- platform plugin integration;
- tax, legal, accounting, or investment advice;
- external attribution such as holiday, region, campaign, or traffic source
  when the data does not contain those fields.

## Target customers

### Best-fit

- Douyin/Kuaishou/Taobao live-commerce merchants with weekly live sessions.
- Small ecommerce teams where the owner, anchor, and operator look at different
  dashboards but lack one clear diagnosis.
- Live-stream creators selling their own products or commission products.
- Agencies or operators that repeatedly answer "why did this session drop?"

### Pain signals

- "GMV looks fine, but cash collected feels wrong."
- "This live room was worse than the previous one, but nobody can say why."
- "Refunds are high, but the team only blames the anchor."
- "We have exports, but no one knows whether the fields are enough."
- "The boss wants a conclusion, but the operator only has screenshots."

### Reject or delay

- The merchant wants guaranteed growth before sharing data.
- The merchant cannot name a concrete business question.
- The merchant only wants generic operating advice.
- The data requires sensitive personal information that is not needed.
- The question depends on dimensions the merchant cannot provide, such as
  traffic source, campaign, region, click, add-to-cart, or dwell time.

## Offer promise

Use this wording:

> 我先不让你发完整后台。你只要给字段名或脱敏样例，我先判断能不能诊断。字段不够，我会直接告诉你缺什么；字段够了，再做一页老板能看懂的归因报告。

The report must answer:

- What happened?
- Which entity caused the change: live session, SKU, anchor, category, field, or
  metric definition?
- What evidence supports it?
- What cannot be concluded yet?
- What exact field or table should be added next?

## Lead scoring

Score every WeChat lead from `0-5`.

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 5 | Has live-session/order exports, concrete drop/refund question, accepts paid diagnosis | Push 199 or 699 immediately |
| 4 | Has data and a real pain, but still needs trust | Offer free field triage, then show sample report |
| 3 | Has pain but unclear data | Ask for table headers only |
| 2 | Curious but no urgent business question | Put in group, nurture with cases |
| 1 | Wants generic advice or free consulting | Send one self-check framework |
| 0 | Requests guarantee, unsafe data, or unrelated advice | Politely reject |

## Xiaohongshu content system

Each post should hit one pain, show one evidence shape, and ask for one keyword.
Keep the path lightweight: comment/DM keyword first, WeChat after the user
shows intent.

### Post 1: GMV 不差，为什么老板还是觉得没赚钱？

Hook:

> 很多直播间最大的问题不是 GMV 掉了，而是"看起来卖了，实际收回来的钱变少了"。

Body:

```text
直播复盘不要只看 GMV。

我更建议先拆 4 个数：
1. 支付 GMV：真正支付了多少钱；
2. GPM：每千次观看带来多少支付；
3. 退款金额：哪些 SKU 把收入吐回去了；
4. 支付转化：观看到支付的链路有没有断。

一个很常见的情况是：
订单数没少，买家数没少，但支付 GMV 掉了。

这时候就别先骂主播，也别先怪流量。
要先看是不是商品结构变了：
高客单 SKU 卖少了，低客单 SKU 卖多了；
或者某个 SKU 退款集中，把现金回收拖下来了。

我现在做的是直播电商 AI 经营诊断：
先看字段够不够，再把问题定位到场次、主播、SKU 或支付口径。
字段不够时，不会硬编结论。
```

### Post 2: 这场直播比上一场差，不一定是没人买

Hook:

> 订单数没变，GMV 也可能掉得很厉害。

Body:

```text
很多团队复盘直播间，会直接问：
"是不是这场没人买？"

但我见过更危险的情况：
支付订单数没变，支付买家数也没变，最后支付 GMV 还是掉了。

这通常说明问题不在"有没有人买"，而在：
1. 买了更便宜的 SKU；
2. 高客单 SKU 没承接住；
3. 支付金额口径变了；
4. 退款或优惠把实际收入吃掉了。

我会先拆：
- 哪场直播；
- 哪个主播；
- 哪些 SKU；
- 支付 GMV 差额；
- GPM 差额；
- 订单数和买家数有没有一起变。

只有拆到实体，复盘才有动作。
```

### Post 3: 退款率高，先别骂主播，先找哪个 SKU 在退

```text

1. 哪个 SKU 退款金额最高；

3. 对应哪场直播；
4. 是否集中在某个主播或品类；
5. 是否需要补充售后原因。
```

## First-customer delivery SOP

1. Confirm the business question in one sentence.
2. Ask for table headers or redacted samples first.
3. Check whether required fields support the question.
4. If fields are enough, collect payment for 199 or 699.
5. Store files in a local customer workspace.
6. Run SME Agent diagnosis and two-session comparison when applicable.
7. Human-review every conclusion for evidence and overclaim risk.
8. Deliver one-page report first.
9. Ask whether the conclusion is useful and what action it triggered.
10. Request permission to publish an anonymized case.

## Campaign success metrics

Minimum viable signal:

- `10` Xiaohongshu DMs or comments with diagnosis keywords.
- `5` WeChat adds with the correct remark.
- `3` users send field headers or redacted samples.
- `1` paid 199 RMB mini diagnosis.

Strong signal:

- `30` qualified DMs/comments.
- `15` WeChat adds.
- `8` field submissions.
- `3` paid mini diagnoses.
- `1` paid 699 RMB standard diagnosis.

Kill or pivot signal:

- Posts get views but no one sends fields.
- Leads want generic advice but no data diagnosis.
- People ask for guaranteed growth.
- Most merchants cannot export payment amount, session ID, or SKU.

If kill/pivot triggers, do not expand marketing. Improve the product wedge or
switch the first paid offer to "field export coaching" before continuing.
