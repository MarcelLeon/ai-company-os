# SME Agent North Star

> Let a small business turn scattered terminology, documents, metrics, warehouse assets, and operating knowledge into answers and actions that are explainable, governable, and maintainable by a small team.

Every product slice must satisfy three constraints:

1. **Business-grounded**: answers resolve to governed terms, metadata, evidence, or explicit uncertainty.
2. **Human-controlled**: consequential tool calls are reviewable, interruptible, and auditable.
3. **Provider-independent**: business behavior does not depend on one LLM, vector store, tool runtime, or user interface.

The first user scenario is an operations analyst asking a business question such as:

> Why did East China revenue decline this month?

The system should resolve the governed metric and dimensions, locate warehouse and knowledge evidence, use approved tools, and return a cited explanation rather than improvise an answer.
